# Idle Grounds — Islands-064

Tribulation Peak P2 terrain tileset for ART-SPEC v6.18. The previously validated flower strip from Islands-047 is preserved byte-for-byte. Rejected terrain files are rebuilt to accepted Center blob, Islands-030 cliff, and Islands-038 18 px sidewall geometry. P3 scatter and rune overlay are intentionally omitted because their prior versions are validated and wait on the Island.

Delivered for review when uploaded; not accepted or integrated.
