Static sprites were generated as new native pixel-art drawings from the Drive reference sprites,
then reduced to the exact target canvas and quantized without dithering to the approved P01-P40
palette. The paper-mill prompt preserved its accepted thatched hut / left water wheel / center
press / right drying rack / rear bamboo composition. The kiln prompt explicitly replaced the
diagonal dragon-kiln silhouette with an upright terracotta kiln, front fire mouth, rear chimney,
brick stack and jars. The workbench prompt explicitly replaced the corner/diamond view with a
front rectangular bench under a folded red awning and four bamboo posts, retaining saw, plane and
plank stack. Every prompt required transparent background, no baked shadow, low noise, large value
groups, selective outline, and minimum 2x2 detail clusters.

The working loops were authored deterministically from the finalized static PNGs so frame 0 remains
exact and stationary pixels do not boil.
