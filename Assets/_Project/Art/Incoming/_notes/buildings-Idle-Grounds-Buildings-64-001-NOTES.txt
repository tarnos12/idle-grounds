Idle Grounds 64-density building pilot

Contents: paper mill, kiln, and workbench static sprites plus four-frame working strips.
All standing-object canvases use 64 pixels per world cell. Animation strips are horizontal.
Frame 0 is byte-for-byte/pixel-for-pixel identical to its matching static sprite.
Frames 1-3 alter only the declared moving mechanisms: mill wheel/press/sheet, kiln flame/smoke,
and workbench saw/chips. Alpha is binary and artwork is restricted to palette P01-P40.

Paper mill follows the previously accepted composition. Kiln and workbench are fresh redraws in
front three-quarter rectangular-footprint projection; the rejected diagonal/corner silhouettes
were not retained. No baked shadows are included.

Status: delivered for owner density/style sign-off; not claimed as accepted or integrated.
