Three independent image-generation studies were used: (1) low varied hazy shan-shui cloud bank, (2) varied billowing midground cumulus with sculpted undersides, (3) sparse asymmetrical wind-stretched foreground wisps. All requested transparent backgrounds, non-repeating silhouettes, ragged forms, and no ellipses/rectangles/flat bands. Final assets were reconstructed to the exact ART-SPEC palette, sizes, alpha steps and seamless boundaries.
