Built-in image generation was used separately for each decoration. Rock prompt: broad top-centre floating-island underside tapering into natural stalactite points, layered earth/brown-grey stone, restrained glowing mineral veins, mist dissolution, transparent background. Roots prompt: ancient twisted giant roots and vines clutching hanging rocks, broad top attachment, tapering downward, sparse jade leaves, transparent background. Outputs were cropped for top-centre attachment, resized to exact canvases, palette-mapped, and alpha-dithered to binary transparency.
