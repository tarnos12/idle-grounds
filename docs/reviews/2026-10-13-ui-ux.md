# UI/UX review of the screenshot tour (2026-10-13)

Reviewer: UI/UX designer (read-only review, no game files touched).
Source: the 100-shot tour (`bg/tour`, 1920x1080, build v53, INDEX.md in the tour folder). I looked at shots 01-12, 14-30, 31, 33, 34, 36, 38, 62, 91, 94 plus the INDEX notes; the other shots are world/coast art with no UI and belong to the art director.
Commitments honoured: `owner-picks-2026-10-12*.json` (select from a menu, all 3 requests open, right-click feeding only, tribute paid at a shrine, no AP/perks/vows) and ADR 0007 (Shrine panel, Request panel, NPC marker, Sky Path, ending on the Heavenly Gate).

Capture caveats (so nobody chases artefacts): cursor/hover/panels are scripted, the OS cursor is not drawn, canvases are Screen Space-Camera. In shots 07/18/26 the name tag ("Kiln") sits over a Storehouse while the highlight is on the tall Kiln; that may be the scripted cursor, but it must be re-checked with a real mouse (finding 11). Shot 36 playtime "497734h" is a headless-clock artefact but the card has no sanity clamp (finding 17).

## Overall verdict

The skin is coherent where it exists: dark navy fill, jade 9-slice frame, gold numerals, one font, the Flow table in Stats and the Pavilion "Carry a Robe to recruit" reason line are the best screens. The weak spots are (1) the UI still tells the pre-ADR-0007 story (steles, "Ascend", "Islands 1/7", Help text), (2) three UI "dialects" side by side (parchment Welcome/Help, dark jade panels, unframed world-space text), (3) emoji icons next to real pixel art, (4) red used for both "primary action" and "danger/problem", (5) world-space labels that are unreadable on pale ground or at far zoom, and (6) a first-run screen that is one wall of instruction text in the corner while the world shows no pointer to where to act.

### P2 (hurts clarity, consistency or flow)

**7. Colour semantics collide: red = primary AND danger** — shots 01, 03, 24, 29, 36 vs 07, 12, 18
- Red-and-gold is used for the primary buttons (Continue, Claim!, Ascend), while red also means problem/blocked ("Needs Clay", "No fuel", locked "?" nodes, Reset, Demolish, Blocked ghost, input counts 0/2). A new player reads "Continue >" as a warning. Gold also means two things in the Altar tree (selected and fully bought, shots 17/31), and the Claim button flips outline colour green to red between 0/5 and 5/5.
- Fix: palette rule (see style rules): primary = bright jade fill with gold text; gold = "value/selected" only; red = destructive/blocked only. Re-colour `ui_btn_primary_*` (art) and keep `UiButtonSkin` roles.
- Effort M (art S + code S). Owner decision: yes, it changes the look the owner signed (Hub Q3).

## HUD clutter vs world visibility

- Quest panel (about 375x570 px = 7.4% of the screen) at first run is the biggest fixed element; at mid game it shrinks (14), which is right. Add the one-line collapsed chip (11) as the default after Quest 5, with a pulse when a quest is claimable.
- Bottom bar plus open dock take up to 20% of the height (15, 21); the build menu hides the buildings you are placing near. Allow the build menu to collapse to a half-height strip while a ghost is held.
- No fixed overlay competes in the corners except top-right; keep that rule: bottom-left = hand chip only, top-centre = toasts, left side free.
- At zoom 6 (13) there is almost no readable UI in the world; see finding 16 for LOD.

## Next to the 3/4 pixel world

Frames, buttons, cards are pixel-consistent (2x slicing). The mismatches are the emoji (flat, glossy, different pixel density), the default-font island labels, the Dragon/ending emoji, and the frameless toast. The in-world counter strips and name tags use a rounded UI font at fractional sizes; they are crisp, but consider the pixel font for labels at 8/16 px multiples so text and sprites share a pixel grid.

## Proposed UI style rule set (for `docs/guides/ui-ux.md` once the owner agrees)

1. **Roles, not one-offs.** Frames: jade = modals and docks; dark = tooltips, chips, toasts and world labels; gold frame = the quest panel only. No parchment.
2. **Colour = meaning.** Jade/green = OK, ready, primary action; gold = value, selection, progress; red = blocked, danger, destructive; amber = waiting, partial; grey = unavailable. Never red for a normal action; never colour alone (add icon or text).
3. **One primary action per panel**, bright jade fill with gold text; the rest are Standard; Close is always top-right and Esc always closes.
4. **Real icons only.** No emoji in shipped UI; item and quest glyphs in text come from a TMP sprite asset made from the world's item icons.
5. **Have/need everywhere** (x/y with the missing number highlighted), with a reason line for anything unavailable.
6. **Fixed screen zones.** Top-right: quest/tribute tracker; top-centre: toasts; bottom: dock and bar; bottom-left: hand chip only; the world centre stays free. Docks never sit under the hand chip.
7. **World labels** use the dark pill + outline, clamp their on-screen size, and drop to icons or hide by zoom (LOD at 2 / 4 / 5).
8. **Type scale on whole pixels:** title 24, body 16, caption 12, numbers in gold; no body text below 12 px at 1080p; one font family.
9. **Tooltips:** every icon-only control and every world object; two lines at hover, expanded after 0.6 s; never under the cursor, never trapping it; keyboard hint inside.
10. **Respect reduce-motion** for every pulse, glint and camera nudge; pixel scaling 1 art px = 2 UI px, no fractional scaling.
