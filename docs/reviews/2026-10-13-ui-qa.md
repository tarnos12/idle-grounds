# UI QA + accessibility review, 2026-10-13

Scope: the 1080p screenshot tour (`bg/tour`, v53, 111 files in the scratchpad `tour/` folder). I viewed all UI shots (01-36, 94, 95, plus 62 for the island label) and spot-checked code by grep (read-only). Nothing was run, edited or committed.

Limits of this review:
- Only 1920x1080 was captured, so the resolution matrix (1280x720, 2560x1440, 16:10, WebGL resize) is **not reviewed**.
- The tour is scripted (debug cursor, direct view calls, no real mouse/keyboard). Hover tags, hand-chip position, scrim and "collapsed quest" results may be capture artefacts; those are marked "verify live".
- Motion/Calm, hover/press states, keyboard focus and cursor art cannot be judged from still frames.
- Contrast numbers are estimates read off the PNGs, not measured.

Severity: P1 = broken/illegible/misleading, fix before the next build. P2 = clear defect or accessibility gap. P3 = polish. Effort: S under 1 h, M a few hours, L a day or more.

## What looks good
- Welcome, Stats and Help body text is 17-20 px with high contrast (dark on cream and light on near-black, roughly 10-15:1).
- Sound/Motion labels change with state ("Muted", "Calm" per `BottomBarView`/`MetaBarView` code), so those toggles are not colour-only.
- Hover tags use text ("No fuel", "Needs Clay"), not just colour.
- Close X on Help/Stats/Altar/Bridge/Pavilion are large (about 36 px) and consistent; Continue/Cancel/Unpair buttons are at least 40 px tall.
- Heading, body and gold-accent hierarchy is consistent across the dialogs.
